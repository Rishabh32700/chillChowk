Questions

Is it really ten minutes?

Within our delivery zone, most orders arrive in about ten minutes. Busy evenings and rain can add a few minutes; the app always shows a live estimate.

Will my drink still be cold?

Yes. Drinks are sealed and packed in insulated bags, and with a ten-minute trip there is no time to warm up.

Why can't I order everything on Brew Rush?

Brew Rush only carries items we can make in under two minutes, so speed never costs quality. The full menu is available for regular delivery and in the café.
